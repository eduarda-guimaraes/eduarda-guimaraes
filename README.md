## 💻 About Me

I am a **QA Tester based in Brazil**, with a technical background in **Full-Stack Development**.

I have experience testing retail management systems, including POS, inventory control, sales flows, digital credit systems, and data analytics modules. I work with manual testing, test scenario creation, business rule validation, database queries, and bug tracking.

My development background helps me understand applications beyond the user interface and collaborate effectively with development teams. I have experience with **Java, TypeScript, React, Node.js, Python, and PL/SQL**.

I enjoy working with Software Quality, but I am also passionate about programming and open to opportunities in both **Quality Assurance and Software Development**.

I will soon begin a degree in **Information Systems** and I am excited to continue learning and growing professionally.

Sou **QA Tester**, com formação técnica e conhecimentos em **Desenvolvimento Full-Stack**.

Tenho experiência testando sistemas de gestão para varejo, incluindo PDV, controle de estoque, fluxo de vendas, crediário digital e módulos de análise de dados. Atuo com testes manuais, criação de cenários, validação de regras de negócio, consultas ao banco de dados e identificação de bugs.
